Koshary Recipe

Description

AAAAHHHHH My Heart. Also One of the MOST BEAUTIFUL Meals in THE WORLD not in the middle east only

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Pasta
  • Brown Lentils
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Crispy Onion
  • Hummus

Steps

  1. Soak the chickpeas for several hours, then boil them. Boil the brown lentils with a little cumin and salt until tender.
  2. Mix two types of pasta (short-cut and spaghetti), boil them in salted water, then drain and toss with a little oil.
  3. Slice the onion thinly, sprinkle with a spoonful of cornstarch and a little flour, and fry in hot, deep oil until golden and crispy.
  4. Sauté the vermicelli in the onion-infused oil until golden, then add the washed rice, salt, and water, and let it cook over low heat.
  5. Sauté the minced garlic in the onion-infused oil; add the tomato juice, vinegar, cumin, salt, and a pinch of sugar, then let the mixture heat up and thicken.
  6. Mix minced garlic with lemon juice, white vinegar, cumin, salt, and a little water, and heat briefly on the stove.
  7. Layer the rice, followed by the pasta and then the lentils; garnish the top with chickpeas and crispy onions, and serve with the sauce and *duqqa* on the side.
